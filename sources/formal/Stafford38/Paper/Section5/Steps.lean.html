20 # The paragraphs of the proof of Theorem 5.7(i)
21 
22 Section 5.2 proves Theorem 5.7(i) by a chain of paragraphs between
23 Lemmas 5.9–5.13.  Each declaration below states the claim of one paragraph and
24 proves it.  `Stafford38.Paper.Section5.Proof` chains them.
25 
26 Notation.  `q : Fin (m+1) → κ[[t]]` is the column `q_i = x̄_i / x̄_j` in the
27 completed discrete valuation ring `V̂ ≅ κ[[t]]`, index `0` is the homogenizing
28 coordinate, the paper's index `1` is `Fin.succ ⟨0, hm⟩`, and `j` is the chart
29 with `q_j = 1`.  `laurentColumn` embeds `κ[[t]]` into `κ((t))`, and
30 `dehomogenizedPoint` is `y = (q_1/q_0, …, q_n/q_0)`.  The matrix
31 `M = (q, Z_{•1}, …, Z_{•d'}, τ)` is `formalTangentMatrix q Z τ`, whose column
32 index `Unit ⊕ (Fin d' ⊕ Unit)` lists `q`, then the `Z`-columns, then `τ`.
33 The row vector `u = (0, 1, 0, …, 0)` is `axisRow (Fin.succ ⟨0, hm⟩)`.
