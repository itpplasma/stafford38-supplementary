9 # The setup paragraph before Lemma 4.8 (tex 672–683)
10 
11 `M = gr Q`, `f : M → M` the action of `x₁`, `V = ker f`, `U = coker f = M/x₁M`,
12 `T = k[x₂,…,xₙ,ξ₂,…,ξₙ]`, with `T[x₁,ξ₁] = k[x,ξ]`.
13 
14 Lean indexes the coordinates from `0`, so paper `n` is Lean `n+1`, and
15 `T = MvPolynomial (Fin n ⊕ Fin n) k` (for paper `n = 1` this is `k`).  `T` acts
16 on `M` through `oldSymbolTangentialAlgEquiv : T ≃ k[x₂,…,ξₙ] ⊆ k[x,ξ]`, and `f`
17 is `oldCoordinateMap`, the `T`-linear action of the symbol `X (inl 0) = x₁`.
