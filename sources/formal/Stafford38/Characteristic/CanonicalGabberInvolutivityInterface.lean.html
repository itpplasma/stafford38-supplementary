8 /-!
9 # Lane B: the residue-extension input reduced to the cited Gabber theorem
10 
11 The adapter
12 `Stafford38.Characteristic.CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControlAdapter`
13 already reduces `CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl` to
14 `CanonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields`: involutivity of every minimal
15 prime of the order initial ideal of the *canonical* quotient, over an
16 arbitrary characteristic-zero field, with no scalar extension, algebraic
17 closure, or Laurent series in the statement.  Two gaps remained between that
18 proposition and the literature theorem it is supposed to instantiate.
19 
20 * Gabber's theorem is about the radical `√Ann_S gr M`, not about the
21   individual minimal primes of `Ann_S gr M`.
22 * Gabber's theorem is about an arbitrary finitely generated filtered module,
23   so neither `0 < N` nor `IsPBWMonicAt` plays any role in it; the canonical
24   right ideal `dA + x₀^N dA` is one instance among all right ideals.
25 
26 This file closes both.  `WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity` is the
27 citation itself, stated for the repository's actual associated graded object:
28 for every characteristic-zero field `L`, every `A_m(L)`, and every right ideal
29 `I`, the radical of `Ann_{S} gr(A/I)` is involutive.  It is *intended* to
30 transcribe the algebraic form of Gabber's theorem proved for
31 almost-commutative `ℚ`-algebras with Noetherian associated graded
32 (Singh--Kumar, *On the Involutivity of the Characteristic Variety*,
33 Comm. Algebra 42 (2014), no. 8, 3607-3618, Theorem 5.1 with Theorems 4.1 and
34 4.2).
35 Two things make that transcription plausible: `A/I` is cyclic, so its
36 order-quotient filtration is good; and the right-module convention costs
37 nothing, because `A/I` is a left `Aᵒᵖ`-module, `gr(Aᵒᵖ) ≅ (gr A)ᵒᵖ = S` since
38 `S` is commutative, and the induced bracket only changes sign.
39 
40 That this Lean proposition really says what the cited theorem says is a human
41 review obligation recorded in `docs/literature-assumptions.yaml`, not
42 something this file establishes.  In particular it rests on the repository's
43 own identification of `OrderAssociatedGradedModule` with `gr(A/I)` for the
44 differential-order filtration.
45 
46 The chain proved here is
47 `WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity →`
48 `WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity →`
49 `RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity →`
50 `CanonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields →`
51 `CanonicalBaseRelativePoissonOverFields →`
52 `CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl`,
53 every step trust-zero.  No Gabber-type statement is proved here; the first
54 proposition is a theorem-shaped interface a caller must still supply.
55 -/

78 /-- **The cited Gabber theorem, stated on the repository's own associated
79 graded module.**  For every characteristic-zero field `L`, every Weyl algebra
80 `A_m(L)` with its differential-order filtration, and every right ideal `I`,
81 the radical of the annihilator of `gr(A/I)` is involutive.
82 
83 `A/I` is cyclic, so its quotient filtration is good; this is therefore the
84 statement of Gabber involutivity, not a project-specific strengthening of it.
85 It is a theorem-shaped interface: no proof is asserted here. -/
86 def WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity : Prop :=
87   ∀ (L : Type u) [Field L] [CharZero L] (m : ℕ)
88     (I : RightIdeal (PresentedWeyl L m)),
89     IsInvolutive
90       (Module.annihilator (SymbolRing L m)
91         (OrderAssociatedGradedModule L I)).radical
92 
93 /-- The same statement written through the order initial ideal, which the
94 repository proves is exactly that annihilator. -/
95 def WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity : Prop :=
96   ∀ (L : Type u) [Field L] [CharZero L] (m : ℕ)
97     (I : RightIdeal (PresentedWeyl L m)),
98     IsInvolutive (orderInitialIdeal L I).radical
99 
100 /-- Involutivity of every minimal prime of the order initial ideal, for an
101 arbitrary right ideal of an arbitrary Weyl algebra over an arbitrary
102 characteristic-zero field. -/
103 def RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity : Prop :=
104   ∀ (L : Type u) [Field L] [CharZero L] (m : ℕ)
105     (I : RightIdeal (PresentedWeyl L m)),
106     ∀ P ∈ (orderInitialIdeal L I).minimalPrimes, IsInvolutive P
107 
108 /-- The annihilator form and the initial-ideal form of the citation are the
109 same proposition. -/
110 theorem weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_iff_associatedGraded :
111     WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity.{u} ↔
112       WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity.{u} := by
113   constructor
114   · intro h L _ _ m I
115     rw [annihilator_orderAssociatedGradedModule]
116     exact h L m I
117   · intro h L _ _ m I
118     have := h L m I
119     rwa [annihilator_orderAssociatedGradedModule] at this
120 
121 theorem weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_of_associatedGraded
122     (h : WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity.{u}) :
123     WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity.{u} :=
124   weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_iff_associatedGraded.mpr h
125 
126 /-- Descent from the radical to its minimal primes.  This is the step that
127 removes the mismatch between the citation and the project interface. -/
128 theorem rightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity_of_radical
129     (h : WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity.{u}) :
130     RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity.{u} := by
131   intro L _ _ m I P hP
132   exact minimalPrimes_isInvolutive_of_radical_isInvolutive_viaLocalization
133     (orderInitialIdeal L I) (h L m I) P hP
134 
135 /-- The canonical right ideal `dA + x₀^N dA` is one right ideal among all of
136 them, so the generic statement specializes to the lane-B target.  Neither
137 `0 < N` nor `IsPBWMonicAt` is used. -/
138 theorem canonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields_of_rightWeyl
139     (h : RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity.{u}) :
140     CanonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields.{u} := by
141   intro L _ _ n N d _ _ P hP
142   exact h L (n + 1) _ P hP
143 
144 /-- Lane B in one step: residue-extension symbol control from the cited
145 Gabber theorem for arbitrary right ideals over arbitrary characteristic-zero
146 fields. -/
147 theorem canonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl_of_associatedGradedRadical
148     (h : WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity.{u}) :
149     CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl.{u} :=
150   canonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl_of_minimalPrimeInvolutivity
151     (canonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields_of_rightWeyl
152       (rightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity_of_radical
153         (weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_of_associatedGraded h)))
