10 /-!
11 # Lane B: the residue-extension input reduced to the cited Gabber theorem
12 
13 The adapter
14 `Stafford38.Characteristic.CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControlAdapter`
15 already reduces `CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl` to
16 `CanonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields`: involutivity of every minimal
17 prime of the order initial ideal of the *canonical* quotient, over an
18 arbitrary characteristic-zero field, with no scalar extension, algebraic
19 closure, or Laurent series in the statement.  Two gaps remained between that
20 proposition and the literature theorem it is supposed to instantiate.
21 
22 * Gabber's theorem is about the radical `√Ann_S gr M`, not about the
23   individual minimal primes of `Ann_S gr M`.
24 * Gabber's theorem is about an arbitrary finitely generated filtered module,
25   so neither `0 < N` nor `IsPBWMonicAt` plays any role in it; the canonical
26   right ideal `dA + x₀^N dA` is one instance among all right ideals.
27 
28 This file closes both.  `WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity` is the
29 citation itself, stated for the repository's actual associated graded object:
30 for every characteristic-zero field `L`, every `A_m(L)`, and every right ideal
31 `I`, the radical of `Ann_{S} gr(A/I)` is involutive.  It is *intended* to
32 transcribe the algebraic form of Gabber's theorem proved for
33 almost-commutative `ℚ`-algebras with Noetherian associated graded
34 (Singh--Kumar, *On the Involutivity of the Characteristic Variety*,
35 Comm. Algebra 42 (2014), no. 8, 3607-3618, Theorem 5.1 with Theorems 4.1 and
36 4.2).
37 Two things make that transcription plausible: `A/I` is cyclic, so its
38 order-quotient filtration is good; and the right-module convention costs
39 nothing, because `A/I` is a left `Aᵒᵖ`-module, `gr(Aᵒᵖ) ≅ (gr A)ᵒᵖ = S` since
40 `S` is commutative, and the induced bracket only changes sign.
41 
42 That this Lean proposition really says what the cited theorem says is a human
43 review obligation recorded in `docs/literature-assumptions.yaml`, not
44 something this file establishes.  In particular it rests on the repository's
45 own identification of `OrderAssociatedGradedModule` with `gr(A/I)` for the
46 differential-order filtration.
47 
48 The chain proved here is
49 `WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity →`
50 `WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity →`
51 `RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity →`
52 `CanonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields →`
53 `CanonicalBaseRelativePoissonOverFields →`
54 `CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl`,
55 every step trust-zero.  No Gabber-type statement is proved here; the first
56 proposition is a theorem-shaped interface a caller must still supply.
57 -/

80 /-- **The cited Gabber theorem, stated on the repository's own associated
81 graded module.**  For every characteristic-zero field `L`, every Weyl algebra
82 `A_m(L)` with its differential-order filtration, and every right ideal `I`,
83 the radical of the annihilator of `gr(A/I)` is involutive.
84 
85 `A/I` is cyclic, so its quotient filtration is good; this is therefore the
86 statement of Gabber involutivity, not a project-specific strengthening of it.
87 It is a theorem-shaped interface: no proof is asserted here. -/
88 def WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity : Prop :=
89   ∀ (L : Type u) [Field L] [CharZero L] (m : ℕ)
90     (I : RightIdeal (WeylAlg L m)),
91     IsInvolutive
92       (Module.annihilator (SymbolRing L m)
93         (OrderAssociatedGradedModule L I)).radical
94 
95 /-- The same statement written through the order initial ideal, which the
96 repository proves is exactly that annihilator. -/
97 def WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity : Prop :=
98   ∀ (L : Type u) [Field L] [CharZero L] (m : ℕ)
99     (I : RightIdeal (WeylAlg L m)),
100     IsInvolutive (orderInitialIdeal L I).radical
101 
102 /-- Involutivity of every minimal prime of the order initial ideal, for an
103 arbitrary right ideal of an arbitrary Weyl algebra over an arbitrary
104 characteristic-zero field. -/
105 def RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity : Prop :=
106   ∀ (L : Type u) [Field L] [CharZero L] (m : ℕ)
107     (I : RightIdeal (WeylAlg L m)),
108     ∀ P ∈ (orderInitialIdeal L I).minimalPrimes, IsInvolutive P
109 
110 /-- The annihilator form and the initial-ideal form of the citation are the
111 same proposition. -/
112 theorem weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_iff_associatedGraded :
113     WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity.{u} ↔
114       WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity.{u} := by
115   constructor
116   · intro h L _ _ m I
117     rw [annihilator_orderAssociatedGradedModule]
118     exact h L m I
119   · intro h L _ _ m I
120     have := h L m I
121     rwa [annihilator_orderAssociatedGradedModule] at this
122 
123 theorem weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_of_associatedGraded
124     (h : WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity.{u}) :
125     WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity.{u} :=
126   weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_iff_associatedGraded.mpr h
127 
128 /-- Descent from the radical to its minimal primes.  This is the step that
129 removes the mismatch between the citation and the project interface. -/
130 theorem rightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity_of_radical
131     (h : WeylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity.{u}) :
132     RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity.{u} := by
133   intro L _ _ m I P hP
134   exact minimalPrimes_isInvolutive_of_radical_isInvolutive_viaLocalization
135     (orderInitialIdeal L I) (h L m I) P hP
136 
137 /-- The canonical right ideal `dA + x₀^N dA` is one right ideal among all of
138 them, so the generic statement specializes to the lane-B target.  Neither
139 `0 < N` nor `IsPBWMonicAt` is used. -/
140 theorem canonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields_of_rightWeyl
141     (h : RightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity.{u}) :
142     CanonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields.{u} := by
143   intro L _ _ n N d _ _ P hP
144   exact h L (n + 1) _ P hP
145 
146 /-- Lane B in one step: residue-extension symbol control from the cited
147 Gabber theorem for arbitrary right ideals over arbitrary characteristic-zero
148 fields. -/
149 theorem canonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl_of_associatedGradedRadical
150     (h : WeylAssociatedGradedRadicalInvolutivity.{u}) :
151     CanonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl.{u} :=
152   canonicalResidueExtensionSymbolControl_of_minimalPrimeInvolutivity
153     (canonicalMinimalPrimeInvolutivityOverFields_of_rightWeyl
154       (rightWeylMinimalPrimeInvolutivity_of_radical
155         (weylOrderInitialRadicalInvolutivity_of_associatedGraded h)))
