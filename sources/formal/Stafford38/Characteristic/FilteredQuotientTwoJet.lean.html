11 /-!
12 # The filtered quotient two-jet module
13 
14 This file constructs the concrete right module over the order-Rees two-jet.
15 The underlying additive quotient is the filtered Rees direct sum modulo the
16 image of the square of the successor shift.  The scalar ring is opposite, so
17 its action retains written right Weyl multiplication.
18 
19 No trace theorem, minimal-prime statement, or Gabber theorem is asserted.
20 -/
