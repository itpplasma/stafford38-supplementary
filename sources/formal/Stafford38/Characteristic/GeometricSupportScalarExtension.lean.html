7 /-!
8 # Reduced order support after scalar extension
9 
10 The terminal Gabber consumer is formulated over a Laurent-series coefficient
11 field.  This file identifies its geometric reduced support with the ordinary
12 reduced order support of the scalar-extended canonical Weyl quotient.  Thus a
13 universal Gabber theorem can be run directly over the extension field; no
14 unrecorded transport of Poisson closure is needed.
15 -/
