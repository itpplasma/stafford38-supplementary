8 /-!
9 # Standard projective charts for component equations
10 
11 For each selected homogeneous coordinate `X chart`, this module identifies
12 the matching affine dehomogenized equation quotient with the quotient of the
13 standard homogeneous localization on `Proj`. The chart is handled by a proved
14 coordinate permutation from the zeroth-chart presentation. It also records
15 equality of the two affine equation ideals on the chart overlap; this module
16 does not identify or glue the integral closures of the affine chart rings.
17 -/
