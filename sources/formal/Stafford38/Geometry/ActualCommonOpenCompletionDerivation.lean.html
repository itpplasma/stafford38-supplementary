14 /-!
15 # Actual common-open and completed-chart derivations
16 
17 The map from the selected point-local ring into the common open is the one
18 induced by `localPointToGenericOpen`, followed by its actual chart-denominator
19 localization.  Its compatibility with the parameter algebra is proved from
20 the localization maps.  Consequently the coordinate derivations on the
21 common open restrict to the prescribed local derivations, and those commute
22 with the canonical multivariate completion and tilted arc on every element.
23 -/
