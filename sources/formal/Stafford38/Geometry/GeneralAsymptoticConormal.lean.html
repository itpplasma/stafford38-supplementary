10 # Asymptotic conormal directions of coordinate-avoiding varieties
11 
12 The boundary witness specializes only in the fibre coordinates. Its base
13 coordinates may have poles. Smooth-locus density and scalar-extension
14 vanishing relate that witness to the ground-field projective direction set.
15 -/
